Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Invitely makes invitation videos on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your phone

  • The invitations you make: names, dates, venues, messages and other details you type.
  • Photos you pick, music you add and voice messages you record. They are copied into the app's private storage and used only to make your video.
  • Your profile (name, family name, city, phone) and settings. They are used only to pre-fill new invitations.
  • A small error log, used only if you choose "Report a problem".

These stay on your phone even when you have an account; they are not uploaded.

Your account

Invitely needs an account so your Premium plan and design unlocks stay with you on every phone. We use Supabase (our sign-in provider) to store:

  • your email address, and your name if you give it or sign in with Google;
  • a password, stored only as a secure hash (never readable), if you sign up with email;
  • an account ID and sign-in times.

With "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email and profile picture address with us; we do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

We use this only to sign you in, to email you sign-in and password-reset codes, and to keep your purchases with your account on every phone. Sign-in data is sent encrypted.

Phone backup

If Android backup is on, your phone's operating system backs up your settings, design unlocks and the text of your invitations to your own Google account, so they come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. Photos, music and voice recordings are not included in this backup. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check which plan you have bought. RevenueCat receives an app user ID (anonymous, or your account ID when you are signed in), your purchase history for this app and basic device information (such as the platform and app version). It does not receive your invitations, photos or profile. Google Play's and RevenueCat's own privacy policies apply to that information.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a voice message in the editor.
  • Photos: only the pictures you choose with the system photo picker.
  • Saving videos: the finished video is saved to your gallery only when you tap "Save to gallery".

Sharing

Videos, photo cards and messages are shared only when you choose to, through the apps you pick (for example WhatsApp).

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

Children

Invitely is meant for adults planning family events and is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

  • Profile → Delete my account permanently deletes your account and sign-in details. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to the support contact shown on our Google Play listing from your account email.
  • Profile → Delete all invitations removes your invitations, photos, music and recordings from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new date. Questions: the support contact shown on our Google Play listing.
