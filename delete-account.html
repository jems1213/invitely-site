Delete your Invitely account

You can delete your Invitely account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete my account. Your account and sign-in details are deleted immediately.
  • Without the app: email the support contact shown on our Google Play listing from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my Invitely account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your email address, name, password hash and account ID. Purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules; cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Invitations, photos and recordings are stored only on your phone; remove them with Profile → Delete all invitations or by uninstalling the app.
